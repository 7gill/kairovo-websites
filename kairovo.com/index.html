Software built for the music room.

Small, careful apps for singers, and for the people who teach them and put them on stage. Made by a singer, for Apple devices, with your data kept on them.

Score Sifter Names the sheet-music PDFs in a singer’s library, and fills in the details forScore reads. FREE · MAC · COMING SOON Recital Atlas Plans recitals and concerts: the repertoire, the performers, the running order and the timings. MAC · COMING SOON Aria Desk Runs a vocal studio, with one teacher or several: students, lessons, repertoire and billing. IN DEVELOPMENT Vocemetry Shows what your voice is doing as you sing: a live pitch trace and a singer’s formant (squillo) analyser, on your iPhone. IPHONE · IN TESTING

How they’re made

five things every Kairovo app keeps to

I.

Your data stays yours

It lives on your own devices. No accounts to make, no servers of mine holding your students, your programmes or your library.

II.

Bought once

No subscriptions. You pay for an app once and it’s yours. Score Sifter is free.

III.

Made for Apple devices, properly

One platform done well rather than three done adequately. Native apps, checked by Apple, that feel at home on your Mac and iPhone.

IV.

Careful with your work

Nothing is changed without your say-so, and what’s done can be undone. Each app is tested hard before it’s offered.

V.

Made by a singer

Each app starts with a real problem from the practice room, the studio or the concert hall.

About

Kairovo is one person: me. I’m a classical singer in New Zealand, and I build the tools I wanted in my own musical life and couldn’t find.

They’re quiet, careful and made to last. If one of them saves you an afternoon, or makes a concert easier to put on, it’s done its job.
