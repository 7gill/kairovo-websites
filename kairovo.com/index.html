§ 01   The suite

Four tools. One philosophy.

Each app stands alone — built around one problem, done well. Together they cover the full desktop workflow for classical voice.

§ 02   Principles

Three things we won't compromise on.

Kairovo is small on purpose. These constraints are load-bearing — they're why the software stays simple and stays yours.

01

Local-first, always

Your data lives on your machine. No accounts required, no internet dependency, no service shutdowns taking your work with them. Your library is yours, indefinitely.

02

One-time pricing

Try the software free for 30 days — fully functional, no restrictions. If it works for you, pay once and use it indefinitely. No subscription, no renewal, no expiry.

03

Built for the real workflow

The features that exist are the ones that real singers and teachers actually need. Nothing is added for a feature list. Nothing is left out to justify a higher tier.

§ 04   Questions

Frequently asked.

Q.01Is Kairovo software really local-first? No cloud at all?+
Yes. Every Kairovo app stores its data in a local SQLite database on your machine. No account is required to use any app. No data is synced to a server. ScoreSifter optionally queries MusicBrainz for works not found in the local catalogue — that's a lookup of a filename, not your data. Everything else is offline.
Q.02What does "one-time pricing" mean in practice?+
Desktop apps (RecitalAtlas, AriaDesk) are free to try for 30 days — fully functional, no feature restrictions, no account required. After 30 days the app stops working until you purchase a licence. The licence is a one-time payment: no subscription, no renewal, no expiry. Updates within the major version are included. Vocemetry is an iOS app sold as a one-time purchase on the App Store — no separate trial period applies. ScoreSifter is permanently free.
Q.03Which platforms are supported?+
ScoreSifter ships for macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel universal), Windows 10/11 x64, and Linux AppImage. RecitalAtlas will ship the same. AriaDesk is native macOS only. Vocemetry is iOS — available as a one-time purchase on the App Store. macOS has the most development time and testing for the desktop apps.
Q.04When is RecitalAtlas available?+
RecitalAtlas is in beta preparation — feature-complete and in testing. It will be available later in 2026. Sign up for the newsletter to get notified when it launches, including early beta access.
Q.05What's the refund policy?+
ScoreSifter is free — nothing to buy. RecitalAtlas and AriaDesk include a fully functional 30-day trial before purchase. Refunds on those are issued case-by-case within 30 days — email hello@kairovo.com with your order number. Full details in the terms.
Q.06Who is behind Kairovo?+
Kairovo is run by Derek, based in New Zealand. He has been singing professionally for 20 years — the apps come directly out of that: noticing the small frustrations that add up across a career and building tools to remove them. Independent studio, no investors, no growth targets.
