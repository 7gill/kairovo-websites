Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Short version: Kairovo apps store all your data on your own devices. The studio website collects minimal data for standard operation. We don’t sell anything to anyone.

The apps

Score Sifter, Recital Atlas, Aria Desk and Vocemetry are local-first applications. All data you enter (students, repertoire, lesson records, file paths) is stored on your own device. The apps do not transmit this data to any server.

Score Sifter has its own privacy policy, which says exactly what it sends when it looks a piece up online: scoresifter.com/privacy.

  • No account required to use any app
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No cloud sync or remote storage
  • No data transmitted without explicit user action

This website (kairovo.com)

Kairovo.com is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare collects basic access logs (IP address, page visited, timestamp) as part of normal operation. These logs are held by Cloudflare under their own privacy policy.

We do not run any analytics scripts, ad tracking or session recording on this site.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the Kairovo newsletter, your email address is stored with Buttondown (our newsletter provider). We use it only to send Kairovo updates. We do not share it with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. Buttondown operates under their own privacy policy.

Buying an app

Paid Kairovo apps are sold on Apple’s App Store: the Mac App Store, and the App Store for iPhone. Apple handles the purchase, the payment and any refund, under Apple’s privacy policy. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your card; Apple tells us only how many copies were sold, and in which countries.

Score Sifter is free, and downloaded directly from scoresifter.com. Downloading it asks only for an email address, as its privacy policy explains.

Children

Kairovo apps and this website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@kairovo.com
