Privacy Policy

Kairovo · Last updated [DATE]

Score Sifter is made by Derek Hill, trading as Kairovo, in New Zealand ("we", "us"). This policy says what Score Sifter and scoresifter.com do with information about you. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and the website keeps only the email address you give it.

The app

Your files stay on your Mac. Score Sifter reads the PDFs in the folder you choose (their names, the text and metadata inside them, and page one) to suggest new names, and renames them only when you ask. Nothing about your files, your library or how you use the app is sent to us or anyone else. The app has no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting.

Looking titles up online. When Score Sifter's own catalogue doesn't know a piece, it asks two public music databases: MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org, run by the MetaBrainz Foundation) and Open Opus (openopus.org). It sends them only the title and composer it is looking for (for example "Ave Maria" and "Schubert"), with a note that the request comes from Score Sifter. Like any website, they see your internet address. Their own privacy policies apply to what they keep: MetaBrainz and Open Opus. You can work entirely offline; the app's catalogue still answers most files.

Reading page one with Apple Intelligence (optional, on Macs that have it). If you turn it on, Apple's on-device model reads page one of a file the catalogue can't find. It runs on your Mac; nothing is sent to Apple or to us.

Updates. The app checks scoresifter.com for a new version (once a day, if you allow it). The check asks for one small file; like any web request, our host (Cloudflare) sees your internet address. The app sends no information about your Mac with it.

The website and the email list

Downloading. To download Score Sifter, you give us your email address. The download starts straight away. We use Buttondown (buttondown.com) to keep the address, and Buttondown sends you one email asking you to confirm that you want to join our list. If you don't confirm, you are not added.

What we keep and why. Only your email address, whether you confirmed, and that you came for Score Sifter. If you confirm, you join Kairovo's one list: occasional news of Score Sifter (updates, fixes, things worth knowing) and of our other apps for singers and teachers. We never sell it, never share it with anyone else, and never use it for advertising.

How long. Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email has an unsubscribe link; one click removes you.

Who else handles it. Buttondown stores the list and sends the emails (Buttondown's privacy policy). Cloudflare hosts the website and the downloads and sees your internet address when you visit, as every host does (Cloudflare's privacy policy). These services may store data outside New Zealand (for example in the United States); we chose them because they protect it to a comparable standard.

Cookies. The website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, at any time, free of charge: write to [CONTACT ADDRESS]. We answer within 20 working days.

New Zealand: you have these rights under the Privacy Act 2020. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Europe and the UK: under the GDPR and UK GDPR, we rely on your consent for the email list (you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing). You also have the right to object, to restrict how we use your information, and to receive it in a portable form, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children. Score Sifter is not aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly keep their email addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with its date; if the change matters, we'll tell the list by email.
