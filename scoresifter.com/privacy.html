Privacy Policy

Kairovo · Last updated April 2026

ScoreSifter is designed with privacy as a default. The app runs entirely on your device — your music files, filenames, and library never leave your computer unless you explicitly trigger an online lookup.

The short version: ScoreSifter collects no personal data, stores nothing off-device, and does not require an account.

What ScoreSifter does not collect

ScoreSifter does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your music library, filenames, or file contents
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, or analytics
  • Device identifiers or IP addresses
  • Any data that persists beyond your local machine

Online lookups (optional)

When ScoreSifter cannot identify a piece from its built-in catalogue, it can optionally query MusicBrainz — a public, community-maintained music database. This lookup sends only the song title and composer name you have entered. No file contents or personal information are sent.

MusicBrainz is an independent service with its own privacy policy. Kairovo has no control over MusicBrainz's data practices. The vast majority of lookups are handled entirely offline by the built-in catalogue.

Website

The ScoreSifter website (scoresifter.com) does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any tracking technology. No personal data is collected by visiting this site.
